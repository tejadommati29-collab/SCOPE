About SCOPE

From a spreadsheet of candidates to a ranked shortlist

SCOPE — Screening Cathodes for Optimal Perovskite Efficiency — is a web workspace for screening perovskite cathode materials with machine learning.

Upload a list of cathode formulas and enter the operating temperature. SCOPE calculates the descriptors from each formula, predicts the remaining properties with built-in SVR models, filters every cathode on TEC, log10(k*) and Ehull, and opens a ranked, exportable shortlist.

Features

Everything you need to screen cathodes

  • Built-in SVR modelsDescriptors are calculated from each formula and properties are predicted – nothing to train.
  • Pass / fail screeningFilter candidates on TEC, log10(k*) kinetics and Ehull stability.

How it works

From dataset to results in four steps

  1. 1Upload and set temperatureCSV or Excel, up to 15 MB, plus the operating temperature. SCOPE detects the formula column and any descriptor columns you already have.
  2. 2Descriptors and predictionsDescriptors are calculated from the formulas; TEC, Ehull and log10(k*) are predicted, as on the single-cathode page.
  3. 3Screening results openEvery cathode is checked and ranked.
  4. 4Predict & exportPredict a single cathode, open any cathode in 3D, see the best in each category and download Excel / CSV.
Screening

How a cathode passes

Thermal expansion (TEC)

Predicted TEC must be at most 15 ×10−6 K−1.

Activity (ORR)

Predicted log10(k*) must be at least −6.40 (log10 of k* in cm/s), the LSC benchmark.

Stability (Ehull)

Predicted energy above hull must be at most 57 meV/atom.

The applicability domain and the precursor cost estimate (Cheap / Moderate / Expensive) are shown for information. They never change PASS / FAIL.
